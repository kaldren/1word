Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 1word ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and stores information when you use the 1word browser extension and associated services.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in and use 1word, we collect:

  • Google account email address — obtained via Google OAuth sign-in and used solely to identify your account.
  • Words you look up — the words you highlight and define are stored in your personal word library so they can be highlighted across pages you visit.
  • Usage counts — we track how many definitions you look up per month to enforce free-tier and Pro-tier limits.

We do not collect browsing history, page content, or any information beyond what is listed above.

2. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To serve and cache word definitions.
  • To display your saved words as underlines across websites you visit.
  • To enforce monthly usage quotas.
  • To process subscription payments via Stripe (Pro plan).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-party services

1word uses the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth — for sign-in. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI — the word you highlight is sent to OpenAI's API to generate a definition. No account information is sent. Governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing for the Pro plan. We never see or store your card details. Governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — infrastructure and database hosting. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

4. Data storage and security

Your account data and word library are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Session tokens are stored locally in your browser's extension storage and are never transmitted to third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

1word is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of 1word after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
