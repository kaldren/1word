Why I built this

I was tired of Googling the same word. Again.

I was reading an article and kept stumbling over the word "procurement". I roughly knew what it meant — but not quite. So I did what I always did: copy the word, open a new tab, type "procurement explain", read the result, close the tab, scroll back to find my place. Every. Single. Time.

I thought — there has to be a better way. Highlight the word once, see what it means. Then the next time you see it on any website, just hover over it and instantly remember. No new tabs. No losing your place. No repetition.

I built 1word for myself, and I kept it simple on purpose. I hate apps packed with a hundred features you never use. This does one thing — makes sure you never get stuck on a word again.
